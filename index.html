Privacy Policy — Call Status

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what the Call Status app ("the app", "we") collects, why, and your choices. The app is provided by Sunny Sudan. For any questions about your data, contact us at anshusudan121@gmail.com.

In short

Call Status lets you share whether you are on a call with people you personally approve, and lets you see the same for people who approve you. We collect the minimum needed for that. We never collect your phone numbers, your contacts, who you are talking to, or the content of any call or notification. We do not sell your data, and we do not show ads.

What we collect

  • An anonymous account ID. When you open the app, it signs in anonymously through Google Firebase. This creates a random ID. It is not linked to your name, email, or phone number.
  • A display name you choose. The name you type so the people you connect with can recognise you.
  • Your share code and the list of people you have approved to see your status, and the people you have asked to follow.
  • Your call status. Whether you are currently on a call ("on a call" or "free"), and the category of app the call is on (for example "WhatsApp", "Phone", "Instagram"). This is a simple on/off indicator plus an app name. We do not collect the number, the contact, the duration of the content, or anything said.

Why we use the Phone and Notification permissions

  • Phone state (READ_PHONE_STATE): to detect when a normal phone call starts and ends. Only the call state (ringing / in a call / idle) is read. Phone numbers are never read.
  • Notification access: to detect ongoing call notifications from apps such as WhatsApp, so the app knows you are on an internet call. The app reads only whether an ongoing call notification exists and which app posted it. It does not read, store, or transmit the text or content of your notifications, and it ignores all non-call notifications.

How your data is shared

Your call status and display name are visible only to the specific people you have approved. No one else can see them. You can remove any approved viewer at any time in the app, which immediately stops their access. Data is stored on Google Firebase (Google LLC) on our behalf; see Google's privacy policy for how Google processes data as our infrastructure provider. We do not share your data with advertisers or sell it to anyone.

Paid feature

Seeing which app a person is calling on is a one-time paid unlock, handled by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your card or payment details; Google processes the payment.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your status is updated in real time and the latest value is kept while you use the app. You can delete your data by removing your approved viewers and uninstalling the app, or by emailing us at [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] to request deletion of your account data. We will delete it within 30 days.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and access is restricted by security rules so that only approved viewers can read your status.

Children

Call Status is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
